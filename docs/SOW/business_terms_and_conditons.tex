\chapter{BUSINESS TERMS / CONDITIONS}

\section{Contract Modifications}
Any changes to the scope, timeline, or budget must be documented in a formal \textbf{Change Request (CR)}. Each CR will be assessed for its impact on the project and must be signed by both parties before implementation.

\section{Confidentiality}
Both parties agree to keep all technical designs, business strategies, and customer data confidential during and after the project duration. The Agency shall not disclose De De Startup’s event data to competitors.

\section{Termination}
Either party may terminate the contract with a 30-day written notice if:
\begin{itemize}
    \item One party fails to meet material obligations defined in this SOW.
    \item The project is delayed beyond 45 days due to unforeseen circumstances.
    \item Upon termination, De De Startup shall pay for all work completed up to the termination date.
\end{itemize}
