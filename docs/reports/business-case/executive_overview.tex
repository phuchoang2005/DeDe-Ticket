\chapter{Executive Summary}
\section{Overview}

The \textbf{ITPJ2602 - Online Event Management \& Ticketing System} is a strategic initiative for \textbf{Dề Dê Startup} to transition from manual, social-media-based operations to a high-performance digital ecosystem. Currently, the organization faces critical business leaks, including revenue loss from counterfeit tickets, frequent system crashes during peak sales (Golden Hours), and a complete lack of customer behavioral data.

The primary objective is to deploy a robust Web Application and a specialized Mobile Scanning Module capable of supporting \textbf{10,000 concurrent users} with sub-2-second response times. Strategically, this project is the cornerstone for Dề Dê’s scalability, aiming to automate the ticketing lifecycle, eliminate fraud via encrypted QR codes, and drive a \textbf{20\% increase} in total revenue. By modernizing the infrastructure, Dề Dê will shift from a reactive manual process to a data-driven, scalable event leader.

\section{Options appraisal}

Three primary options were evaluated to address the current bottlenecks:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Option 1: Status Quo (Manual Processing):} Rejected due to high risks of fraud, inability to scale, and continued loss of brand reputation during system outages.
    \item \textbf{Option 2: Third-party SaaS Integration:} Rejected because of high long-term commission fees, lack of customization for ``offline-mode'' scanning requirements, and limited control over sensitive customer data.
    \item \textbf{Option 3: Custom Build (Preferred):} Selected as it allows for a tailored high-concurrency architecture (handling 10k CCU) and integrated seat mapping. This option provides full ownership of data and the flexibility to implement specific anti-fraud and retry mechanisms, ensuring the best ROI over the startup's growth trajectory.
\end{itemize}

\section{Costs and benefits}

\textbf{Main Costs:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Total Budget:} 1.5 Billion VNĐ.
    \item \textbf{Timeline:} 5 months of development using Scrum (Incremental/Iterative).
    \item \textbf{Human Resources:} A dedicated 5-member agile team.
    \item \textbf{Infrastructure:} Cloud-based servers optimized for high-load simulation.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Main Benefits:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Financial:} Expected 20\% growth in revenue through improved reach and eliminated ticket duplication.
    \item \textbf{Operational:} Rapid check-in process ($<$ 5s per person) and 98\% successful transaction rate.
    \item \textbf{Strategic:} Real-time reporting and analytics for 15-20 annual events; 100\% elimination of manual bank transfer verification errors.
    \item \textbf{Customer Experience:} Reduced friction during ``Golden Hour'' sales and guaranteed ticket authenticity.
\end{itemize}
