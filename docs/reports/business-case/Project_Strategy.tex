\chapter{Project strategy}

The project will follow the \textbf{Scrum (Agile)} framework, utilizing an \textbf{Incremental and Iterative} delivery model. This ensures that the high-risk features (Load handling and Payment) are tested early and often.

\section*{Key Milestones and Deliverables}

As per the project requirements and the 5-month timeline, the key milestones are structured around the three core pillars: \textbf{System Foundation (Web), Mobile Integration (Scanning), and Performance Validation (High Load)}.
