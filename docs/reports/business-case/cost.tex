\section{Projected Benefits and Savings}
The benefits are calculated based on Dề Dê’s current scale of \textbf{20 events/year} and an average ticket price of \textbf{500,000 VNĐ}.

\subsection*{A. Revenue Growth (Target: +20\%)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Manual sales are limited by system crashes and slow response.
    \item \textbf{Future State:} High-load stability (10k CCU) allows for ``Golden Hour'' flash sales, capturing impulsive buyers.
    \item \textbf{Calculation:} If total annual revenue is 50 Billion VNĐ, a 20\% increase equals \textbf{10 Billion VNĐ} in additional gross revenue.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{B. Fraud Prevention \& Loss Reduction}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Loss due to ticket duplication and manual bank transfer errors (estimated at 2\% of revenue).
    \item \textbf{Future State:} 100\% unique encrypted QR codes and automated payment verification.
    \item \textbf{Savings:} 2\% of 50 Billion VNĐ = \textbf{1 Billion VNĐ/year}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{C. Operational Efficiency (Labor Savings)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Requires 10+ admins to verify transfers and 20+ staff for manual check-in at gates.
    \item \textbf{Future State:} Automated verification and rapid mobile scanning ($<$ 5s).
    \item \textbf{Savings:} Reduction of seasonal staff costs by approximately \textbf{200 Million VNĐ/year}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Financial Evaluation: NPV, ROI and Payback Analysis}

To evaluate the financial feasibility of the project, a discounted cash flow analysis is performed including Net Present Value (NPV), Return on Investment (ROI), and Payback Period. This approach follows standard project financial evaluation practices recommended in project management frameworks such as PMBOK.

\subsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Initial Project Investment: \textbf{1.5 Billion VNĐ}
    \item Expected Annual Benefits:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Increased ticket revenue from better capacity utilization: \textbf{10 Billion VNĐ}
            \item Fraud prevention (fake ticket reduction): \textbf{1 Billion VNĐ}
            \item Operational cost savings (automation): \textbf{0.2 Billion VNĐ}
        \end{itemize}
    \item Total Annual Benefit: \textbf{11.2 Billion VNĐ}
    \item Discount Rate (Cost of Capital): \textbf{10\%}
    \item Project Evaluation Horizon: \textbf{3 years}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Net Present Value (NPV)}

The Net Present Value is calculated using the discounted cash flow formula:

\begin{center}
    {\Large
    \[
    NPV = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{B_t - C_t}{(1+r)^t}
    \]
    }
\end{center}

Where:

\begin{itemize}
    \item $B_t$ = Benefit in year $t$
    \item $C_t$ = Cost in year $t$
    \item $r$ = Discount rate
    \item $n$ = Project evaluation period
\end{itemize}

Assuming constant annual benefits of \textbf{11.2 Billion VNĐ}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Year 1 PV = $\frac{11.2}{1.1}$ = 10.18 Billion VNĐ
    \item Year 2 PV = $\frac{11.2}{1.1^2}$ = 9.25 Billion VNĐ
    \item Year 3 PV = $\frac{11.2}{1.1^3}$ = 8.41 Billion VNĐ
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Total Present Value of Benefits}

\[
PV = 10.18 + 9.25 + 8.41 = 27.84 \text{ Billion VNĐ}
\]

\[
NPV = 27.84 - 1.5 = 26.34 \text{ Billion VNĐ}
\]

\textbf{Result:} The project produces a strongly positive NPV, indicating high financial attractiveness.

\subsection{Return on Investment (ROI)}

OI is calculated based on discounted benefits and discounted costs.

\begin{center}
{\Large
\[
ROI = \frac{Total\ Discounted\ Benefits - Total\ Discounted\ Costs}{Total\ Discounted\ Costs}
\]
}
\end{center}

\textbf{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Initial investment cost: \textbf{1.5 Billion VNĐ}
\item Annual benefit: \textbf{11.2 Billion VNĐ}
\item Discount rate: \textbf{10\%}
\item Project evaluation period: \textbf{3 years}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Present Value of Benefits}

\begin{itemize}
\item Year 1: $ \frac{11.2}{1.1} = 10.18$ Billion VNĐ
\item Year 2: $ \frac{11.2}{1.1^2} = 9.25$ Billion VNĐ
\item Year 3: $ \frac{11.2}{1.1^3} = 8.41$ Billion VNĐ
\end{itemize}

\[
Total\ Discounted\ Benefits = 27.84 \text{ Billion VNĐ}
\]

\[
Total\ Discounted\ Costs = 1.5 \text{ Billion VNĐ}
\]

\[
ROI = \frac{27.84 - 1.5}{1.5} = 17.56
\]

\[
ROI = 1756\%
\]

\textbf{Interpretation}

\begin{itemize}
\item ROI is positive and significantly greater than zero.
\item The project generates a return far exceeding the initial investment.
\item Therefore, the project is financially attractive.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Discounted Payback Period}

The Discounted Payback Period measures the time required for the project to recover its initial investment considering discounted cash flows.

\textbf{Step 1: Calculate discounted cash flow}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
Year & Cash Flow (Billion VNĐ) & Discounted Cash Flow & Cumulative DCF \\
\hline
0 & -1.5 & -1.5 & -1.5 \\
1 & 11.2 & 10.18 & 8.68 \\
2 & 11.2 & 9.25 & 17.93 \\
3 & 11.2 & 8.41 & 26.34 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Step 2: Identify payback year}

The investment becomes positive during the first year.

\textbf{Step 3: Apply DPP formula}

\[
DPP = Year\ before\ recovery +
\frac{Remaining\ Investment}{Discounted\ Cash\ Flow\ of\ Recovery\ Year}
\]

\[
DPP = 0 + \frac{1.5}{10.18}
\]

\[
DPP \approx 0.147\ years
\]

\[
DPP \approx 1.76\ months
\]

\textbf{Conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
\item Discounted ROI: \textbf{1756\%}
\item Discounted Payback Period: \textbf{≈ 1.8 months}
\end{itemize}

This indicates that the project can recover its investment very quickly and generate substantial financial value.
