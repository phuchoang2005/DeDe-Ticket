\section{Projected Benefits and Savings}
The benefits are calculated based on Dề Dê’s current scale of \textbf{20 events/year} and an average ticket price of \textbf{500,000 VNĐ}.

\subsection*{A. Revenue Growth (Target: +20\%)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Manual sales are limited by system crashes and slow response.
    \item \textbf{Future State:} High-load stability (10k CCU) allows for ``Golden Hour'' flash sales, capturing impulsive buyers.
    \item \textbf{Calculation:} If total annual revenue is 50 Billion VNĐ, a 20\% increase equals \textbf{10 Billion VNĐ} in additional gross revenue.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{B. Fraud Prevention \& Loss Reduction}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Loss due to ticket duplication and manual bank transfer errors (estimated at 2\% of revenue).
    \item \textbf{Future State:} 100\% unique encrypted QR codes and automated payment verification.
    \item \textbf{Savings:} 2\% of 50 Billion VNĐ = \textbf{1 Billion VNĐ/year}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{C. Operational Efficiency (Labor Savings)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Current State:} Requires 10+ admins to verify transfers and 20+ staff for manual check-in at gates.
    \item \textbf{Future State:} Automated verification and rapid mobile scanning ($<$ 5s).
    \item \textbf{Savings:} Reduction of seasonal staff costs by approximately \textbf{200 Million VNĐ/year}.
\end{itemize}

\section{ROI and Payback Period}
To evaluate the financial viability, we use the Return on Investment (ROI) formula for the first year of operation:

\begin{center}
    {\Large
    \[ ROI = \frac{TotalBenefits - TotalCosts}{TotalCosts} \times 100 \]
    }
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Total Benefits (Year 1):} 10B (Growth) + 1B (Fraud Prevention) + 0.2B (Ops Savings) = \textbf{11.2 Billion VNĐ}.
    \item \textbf{Total Investment:} 1.5 Billion VNĐ.
    \item \textbf{ROI (Year 1):} $\approx$ \textbf{646\%}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Conclusion:} The project is highly lucrative. The ``Payback Period'' is estimated to be less than \textbf{2 months} after the official launch, considering the high volume of tickets processed per event (50,000 tickets).
